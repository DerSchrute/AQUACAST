RESEARCH SANDBOX

Model evaluation

Validate monthly records, compare three transparent forecasting methods, and inspect one-step-ahead errors.

!
Not a validated operational modelFictional demonstration results do not establish predictive accuracy. Imported CSV stays in this browser session; do not upload confidential records to an unapproved static prototype.

Import & validation

CSV headers: station,period,consumption · YYYY-MM · nonnegative m³ · one row per station-month

Choose a local CSV or load synthetic demonstration data. Invalid dates, duplicate station-months, and negative values are rejected. Gaps in monthly sequences prevent evaluation until resolved.

No dataset loaded.

Evaluation protocol

Rolling-origin, one-month-ahead evaluation starts after six consecutive training months. Naïve, 3-month moving average, exponential smoothing (α=0.30), naïve with drift, and linear trend use the same test periods. A challenger is selected only if its MAE is at least 5% lower than the naïve baseline. This threshold is a provisional heuristic, not proof of generalization. MAE and RMSE use m³; MASE uses the in-sample naïve difference scale. A zero denominator produces N/A. This sandbox ranks models on the evaluation period; an independent holdout is still needed for an unbiased final accuracy claim.

Forecast methodology

Research-dependent

The final approach should follow dataset inspection and a defensible evaluation process. A simple baseline should be considered before introducing more complex models.

  • Inspect data frequency, completeness, and observation length.
  • Establish a suitable baseline and time-aware validation.
  • Compare candidate methods using appropriate error metrics.
  • Report limitations and avoid claiming unsupported accuracy.
